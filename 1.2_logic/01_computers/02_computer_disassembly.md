## Internal Layout
Open the computer case and observe the internal components.
Describe what you see.

After opening the computer case, I could see the internal components and how they were arranged. The motherboard connects the main parts of the computer. I looked for the RAM, storage drive, cooling fan and power supply. Each component has its own job, and all of them work together to make the computer function. This activity helped me understand the hardware better than just reading about it.
